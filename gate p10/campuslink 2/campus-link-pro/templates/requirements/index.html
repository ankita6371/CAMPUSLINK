System capabilities

Functional requirements

Features and user-visible behavior the application must provide.

FR-01

Authentication and role access

The system shall allow authorized users to sign in and sign out, assign administrator, placement officer, and student roles, and restrict pages and actions according to role.

Access control
FR-02

Student records

Placement staff shall create, view, update, search, and manage student profiles, including academic details, branch, skills, placement status, and certificates.

Student management
FR-03

Company and recruiting partner records

Placement staff shall maintain company profiles, locations, recruiting information, and the roles and skills requested by each company.

Recruiter management
FR-04

Job drives and eligibility

The system shall record drive dates, application deadlines, required skills, eligibility rules, and determine whether drives are upcoming, active, or closed from their dates.

Drive lifecycle
FR-05

Candidate matching and skill gaps

The system shall compare student profiles with drive criteria, show explainable match results, identify each candidate’s missing required skills, and recommend relevant skill development.

Candidate matching
FR-06

Applications and interviews

Placement staff shall record and track student applications, selection progress, interview schedules, interview status, and related outcomes.

Placement workflow
FR-07

Resume creation and download

Students shall maintain their own resume information, see whether it needs generation or is ready, and download a generated resume when required sections are complete.

Student self-service
FR-08

Mock tests

Students shall start category-based mock tests, answer randomized question sets, submit attempts, and review saved results and explanations.

Practice and assessment
FR-09

Interview practice

Students shall access interview preparation and practice features associated with their placement opportunities.

Career preparation
FR-10

Offers and placement outcomes

Placement staff shall record offers, update offer decisions, and review placement outcome summaries.

Offer tracking
FR-11

Centralized communications

Placement staff shall send announcements to the intended audience; students shall read messages and ask questions in private threads; staff shall reply and track responses.

Messaging
FR-12

Dashboard, notifications, analytics, and reports

The system shall summarize placement activity, notify staff of relevant workflow updates, provide analytics, and allow placement reports to be viewed or exported.

Insights and reporting
Quality attributes

Non-functional requirements

Standards for security, usability, reliability, and operating the system.

01

Security and privacy

Passwords shall be stored as one-way hashes. Authenticated routes shall enforce role permissions, and student-specific records shall only be accessible to their owner and authorized staff.

Security
02

Performance

On the intended deployment, normal page requests should complete within 3 seconds under typical campus usage. Long-running exports should provide a clear download response.

Response time target
03

Ease of use

Common placement tasks should use clear labels, validation feedback, consistent navigation, and responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.

Usability
04

Reliability and data integrity

Record updates shall be validated and persisted consistently. Failed database operations shall report an error without showing a success state.

Reliability
05

Accessibility

Controls should be keyboard operable, labeled for assistive technology, and maintain readable contrast and visible focus indicators.

Accessibility target
06

Compatibility

The application should work in current mainstream desktop and mobile browsers and adapt to commonly used screen widths.

Browser support
07

Maintainability

Application logic, database access, templates, and styling should remain organized into understandable modules so features can be tested and updated safely.

Maintainability
08

Backup and recovery

Production operators should back up the database regularly and verify that a backup can be restored before relying on it for recovery.

Operations

Performance and accessibility values above are project targets; verify them on the final deployment and assistive-technology setup.
