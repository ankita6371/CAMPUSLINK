# CAMPUSLINK

### AI-Powered Campus-to-Corporate Placement Management System

CAMPUSLINK is a web-based placement management platform designed to
help colleges manage students, companies, job drives, eligibility,
applications, interviews, offers, and placement analytics in one
centralized system.

---

## Features

- Student Management
- Company Management
- Job Drive Management
- Automatic Eligibility Checking
- AI-Based Candidate Matching
- Application Tracking
- Application stages: Applied, In Progress, Selected, and Rejected
- Interview Scheduling
- Offer Management
- Resume builder with PDF export and original PDF/DOCX uploads (up to 10 MB)
- Placement Analytics
- Reports and Data Export
- Secure Authentication
